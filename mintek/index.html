Our Process


  Step 1

First meeting: Every client's needs are different. Are you a small business? Or maybe a new franchise? Our expert representatives sit down with you to understand your digital needs, your business's pain points and help determine how to best achieve your goals.


  Step 2

Kick Off: After the proposal has been accepted, you'll be introduced to your dedicated project manager. Your PM will help collect all necessary public required to bring your website to life, from images to documents. They will also help explain you the project timeline and milestones to expect while we work on your website.


  Step 3

Research and Design: We put time into understanding your market, competition and target audience before writing a single line of code. Armed with that knowledge and our own expertise, we craft pixel-perfect designs for desktop and mobile that will make your website sparkle and shine!


  Step 4

Development: Here comes the meat of the project. Once the design has been approved, we go to work converting whats on paper into fully functioning web pages. We provide customers with weekly updates and incorporate their feedback continuously into the final design.


  Step 5

Testing and Launch: Aaaand blast off! We move the website off our development server and onto your own hosting domain and test every corner case to ensure your website launches smoothly. Once we get the green light, the curtains comes down and your new website is ready to be seen by the entire world!


  Step 6

Post Launch: Your shiny new website is up for the world to see. Now what? What if you want to make a change to the pricing of one of your menu items? We will provide complimentary virtual basic training to our clients on how to make changes to your website to make sure it is always up-to-date with latest information. We may also provide you with documentation for daily functionality of the website. And if a problem ever occurs, our team is always available through e-mail or cellphone to guide you through the problem.
